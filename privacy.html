Privacy Policy

Puzzle Collection · Last updated: 2026-10-02

Puzzle Collection ("the app") is a collection of single-player offline puzzle games: Water Sort, Number Merge, Sudoku, Block Puzzle, Color Connect, Word Guess, Mines and Solitaire. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

No account, no server

The app does not ask you to sign in and does not have a backend server. There is nothing for the developer to collect, because nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Your progress — coin balance, which level you're on in each game, your best scores, your settings, and any level you left unfinished — is saved only on your own device, using Android's standard app storage. It is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

You can erase this at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the app, or by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage from your device's system settings.

Advertising and in-app purchases

The version of the app you are using today shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. A future update is planned to add:

  • Optional banner and interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads for in-game helpers, served through Google AdMob; and
  • A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and any rewarded-ad coin purchases, processed through Google Play's billing system.

When that update ships, Google AdMob may collect and use device and advertising identifiers to show and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app will ask for your consent to personalised ads before any ad request is made, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You will be able to change that choice at any time from the app's "Privacy options" setting. In-app purchases are billed and processed entirely by Google Play; the developer never sees your payment details.

This policy will be updated, and the version in effect at the time will say so, before that update reaches you.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.
